Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026.

This policy describes information associated with visits to Cozy Green Nook, how it may be used, and choices available to visitors. As of this revision, the reviewed page templates contain no installed Google AdSense ad tags or analytics integrations. It may display ads served by Google AdSense in the future. If advertising is enabled, this policy will describe the relevant data and cookie practices before or when that change is made.

Information that may be collected

Server and security logs. When a browser requests a page, the hosting provider may process technical request information such as an IP address, browser and device details, requested URL, date and time, and error or security information. These records help deliver the site, troubleshoot failures, and protect the service. The site does not control the hosting provider’s independent infrastructure logs.

Contact messages. If you submit the contact form, your name, email address, and message are sent to Web3Forms for delivery. Information in the message is used to read and respond to the inquiry. Do not submit sensitive personal information. The form provider may process technical data needed to deliver and secure the submission; see the provider’s privacy information for its practices.

Preferences. The cookie notice stores your choice in browser local storage so it can remember whether you accepted or rejected non-essential technologies. Local storage is kept in your browser and is not an advertising cookie. You can remove it through the browser’s site-data controls or use the “Cookie Settings” link in the footer to change your choice.

Advertising and third-party cookies

The site may display ads served by Google AdSense. If that service is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies, including cookies associated with Google or DoubleClick domains, to deliver ads, limit repeated ads, measure ad performance, or personalize advertising according to the applicable settings and consent choices. Advertising tags are not installed on this site at the time of this update. Google explains how AdSense uses cookies.

Visitors can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. They can also visit Digital Advertising Alliance WebChoices to learn about opt-out choices from participating companies. These controls do not necessarily stop all advertising or remove every cookie; they affect the participating service and browser or account settings described by those providers.

California privacy choices

If the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), applies to a visitor and the site’s activities, the visitor may have rights to know or access, delete, or correct certain personal information; to opt out of sale or sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising; and to receive equal service for exercising applicable rights. To submit a request, email contact@cozygreennook.com with “California privacy request” in the subject and describe the request. We may need to verify the request and will respond as required by applicable law. The California Privacy Protection Agency explains consumer rights in its CCPA FAQ.

Children, external links, and retention

Cozy Green Nook is not directed to children under 13, and the site does not knowingly request personal information from children under 13. The site links to third-party websites whose privacy practices it does not control; review their policies before submitting information there.

Contact information and messages are retained only as needed to handle the inquiry, maintain relevant records, and meet applicable obligations. Hosting and form providers may retain technical or submission records under their own policies and retention practices.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when site functions or applicable requirements change. The current revision date appears at the top of this page. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to contact@cozygreennook.com.
