A place to learn at your own pace

Cozy Green Nook shares practical advice for caring for plants and growing food at home. Every plant and growing space is different; our guides help you observe conditions and make informed choices.

When appropriate, our editorial guides draw on published sources and distinguish general advice from plant-specific needs. We do not present undocumented hands-on experience as fact. Learn about our research and review process.
