About Cozy Green Nook

Practical, source-based guidance for people growing houseplants, food, and garden plants in the spaces they have.

Our purpose and audience

Cozy Green Nook is an independent editorial site about home gardening. Its guides are written for readers who want clear starting points for caring for indoor plants, growing vegetables and herbs in containers, planning a balcony garden, or understanding a plant problem. The site is intended to help readers make informed choices with the light, space, time, and equipment available to them.

Gardening advice is rarely identical for every household. Species, cultivar, container, growing medium, climate, season, water quality, and exposure can all change what a plant needs. Our articles explain the conditions behind a recommendation wherever practical, so a reader can decide whether it fits their own setting rather than treating a calendar or rule of thumb as universal.

How our guides are researched

We build guides from published horticultural information and identify sources that readers can consult directly. Depending on the subject, source review may include university Cooperative Extension publications, botanical garden references, plant-care resources from public institutions, and the ASPCA’s Toxic and Non-Toxic Plants database when discussing pet toxicity. A source is used for the specific point it supports; it is not presented as a blanket endorsement of every statement in an article.

We summarize source material in original wording and distinguish general guidance from species-specific information. Plant names can be ambiguous, advice can depend on local climate, and product instructions can change. For that reason, articles direct readers to verify a plant’s botanical identity, follow the product label, and consult a local extension office or qualified professional when identification or conditions require it.

Review and editorial standards

Articles are reviewed for relevance to their stated question, clarity, internal consistency, useful headings, and appropriate source attribution. We look for advice that is too broad, unsupported claims, confusing instructions, and repetition that does not help the reader. When a topic involves pets, we avoid treating common names as definitive and point readers to the ASPCA database and veterinary advice.

Cozy Green Nook does not claim personal growing trials, original field research, professional credentials, or firsthand results that have not been documented. The guides are editorial summaries based on cited or otherwise identified published references, not reports of experiments performed by the site.

Corrections and updates

We welcome corrections, clear source suggestions, and notices about links or instructions that appear outdated. When a reader identifies a specific issue, the editorial review checks the wording against relevant sources and the context of the article. If a change is justified, the affected page is revised; we do not promise a particular outcome or turnaround for every suggestion. Review dates are updated when a page is substantively reviewed, not simply because a site-wide edit occurred.

Contact

For a correction or question about an article, use the Contact page or email contact@cozygreennook.com. Please include the article URL and the specific sentence or claim you are asking us to review. For the editor profile and editorial process, see Editorial Team.
