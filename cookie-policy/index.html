Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026.

This policy explains cookies and similar browser storage used by Cozy Green Nook. At the time of review, the site build contains no active advertising or analytics integration. It may display ads served by Google AdSense later; if advertising is enabled, advertising cookies may be used as described below and in the Privacy Policy.

What the site uses now

The consent notice stores your choice in local storage in your browser. Local storage is used to remember whether you accepted or rejected non-essential technologies and to avoid showing the notice on each page. It is not an advertising cookie. The hosting provider may also process technical request data in server logs; those logs are described in the Privacy Policy.

Advertising cookies if ads are enabled

If Google AdSense is enabled, Google may use first-party and third-party cookies to serve, measure, and personalize advertising, depending on the ad configuration, visitor settings, and consent. Third-party advertising cookies may be associated with Google or DoubleClick domains. The site’s notice offers “Accept” and “Reject non-essential” choices. At present, no advertising tag is installed, and choosing either option does not load an ad script.

You can also manage personalized ads in Google Ads Settings and review industry opt-out choices at aboutads.info. Those controls are managed by third parties and may not remove all cookies.

Manage or change your choice

Use “Cookie Settings” in the site footer to reopen the consent notice and change your preference. You can clear local storage and cookies through your browser’s privacy or site-data settings. Blocking all storage may affect the site’s ability to remember your preference, though the public reading pages remain available.
