Plant care and growing conditions

Plant responses vary by species, cultivar, light, climate, soil, water quality, and local conditions. Recommendations are general starting points. Confirm plant identity, follow product labels, and consult a local expert when identification or a site-specific decision is needed.

Pets and plant toxicity

Toxicity depends on the exact species and may vary by plant part and amount ingested. Common names can refer to different plants. Confirm the botanical identity in the ASPCA Toxic and Non-Toxic Plants database and ask a veterinarian about a specific pet or possible ingestion. ASPCA notes that its list is not all-inclusive, so absence from the list is not proof of safety.
