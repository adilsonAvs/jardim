Plant care and growing conditions

Plant responses vary with species, cultivar, light, climate, soil, water quality, and local conditions. Recommendations are general starting points; check the needs of the exact plant and adjust based on observation. Follow product labels and local rules when using fertilizers, pesticides, or other garden products.

Pets and plant toxicity

Plant toxicity can vary by species and by the plant part or amount ingested. Common names can refer to different plants. Confirm the botanical identity and current safety information with the ASPCA Animal Poison Control plant database and your veterinarian. If a pet may have eaten a plant and is unwell, contact a veterinarian promptly.
