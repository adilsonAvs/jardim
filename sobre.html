About Cozy Green Nook

Cozy Green Nook is an editorial project about plants and home gardening. We create practical guides for people who want to start growing or care for a green space, whether it is a windowsill, balcony, or yard.

How We Work

We explain gardening concepts in clear language, with practical steps and attention to the differences among plants and growing conditions. Our advice is general; climate, cultivar, light, soil, season, and local conditions can all affect the care a plant needs.

Editorial Standards

We do not claim credentials we do not have or promise results. We review content for clarity and avoid risky instructions. When a topic calls for expert identification or local guidance, we say so.

Research and Sources

Our guides are based on published horticultural references, including university extension resources. Each article links to further reading so readers can check the source behind its recommendations. We summarize those sources in our own words, note when advice depends on plant or growing conditions, and do not describe source-based guidance as testing conducted by Cozy Green Nook. We welcome corrections and suggestions for relevant references.

Author and Editorial Responsibility

Cozy Green Nook is edited by Adilson V. Santos. The site does not claim unverified professional credentials or first-hand tests. For editorial questions or corrections, email adilsonvsantos@gmail.com.
