Potting Mix Volume Calculator

Estimate how much growing mix fits inside a container. Enter the usable inside dimensions and the depth you plan to fill.

Container dimensions

Use inside measurements and the actual fill depth, not the container’s full outside height. Leave room at the top for watering.

How the estimate is calculated

A round container is estimated as a cylinder: π × radius² × fill depth. A rectangular pot or window box is estimated as length × width × fill depth. The dimensions are converted to cubic centimeters and divided by 1,000 to get liters; liters are converted to US liquid quarts by dividing by 0.946352946.

The calculator treats the container as a simple geometric shape. Tapered pots, curved walls, rounded corners, root balls, and headspace reduce the amount of mix that will actually fit. Treat the result as a planning estimate and buy a little less if the container narrows toward the bottom.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I measure the inside or outside of the pot?

Measure the inside opening and interior depth. Outside dimensions overstate the space available for growing mix.

Why is the amount different from the bag label?

The calculator uses a simple geometric volume. Taper, rounded corners, headspace, and root balls reduce the fill volume, while bag sizes may be rounded.

How many quarts are in a liter?

One liter is about 1.057 US liquid quarts. This calculator uses that conversion; it does not use Imperial quarts.

Can I use the estimate for garden soil?

The tool estimates volume only. Choose a growing medium suited to the plant and container; it does not determine which ingredients are appropriate.
