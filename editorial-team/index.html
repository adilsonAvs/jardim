Editorial Team

Adilson V. Santos, Editor

Adilson V. Santos is the editor of Cozy Green Nook. The site publishes home-gardening guides based on published references and does not claim personal growing tests or professional credentials.

Editorial work includes defining each guide’s subject, checking that its practical advice answers that subject, reviewing wording for clarity, and checking source links and claims. The goal is to make recommendations understandable while preserving important conditions such as plant identity, light, season, container, and local climate.

Sources and research policy

Research uses relevant published horticultural references. Depending on the subject, these may include university Cooperative Extension publications, botanical garden resources, and the ASPCA’s plant toxicity database. Sources are linked in articles when they support a specific recommendation. We do not present external sources as original experiments by Cozy Green Nook.

Where guidance differs by species or setting, an article should explain that limitation or direct readers to a suitable local professional. Pet toxicity statements should be checked against the plant’s scientific name in the ASPCA Toxic and Non-Toxic Plants database; a veterinarian should advise on a possible ingestion.

Corrections and review date

Readers can report an error or suggest a source through the Contact page or by email at contact@cozygreennook.com. Please include the page URL and the detail to review. Suggestions are checked against the article’s scope and supporting references; a correction is made when the review supports changing the page.
