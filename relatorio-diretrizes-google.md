# Review of Official Google Guidance

Reviewed October 5, 2026. The 31 official Google links below were opened. Some help pages returned only metadata or partial content, so this report does not claim a complete review of those pages. All links point to their official domains.

## Principles reflected in the site

- Publish original, useful content and make navigation clear.
- Avoid incentivized clicks, deceptive ad placements, and artificial traffic. This version has no live ads or calls to click.
- The project specifications for 40 articles, 1,000 words, and three images are editorial goals, not official AdSense requirements or approval guarantees.
- Structured data describes visible article content and does not invent reviews. Article pages use BlogPosting markup.
- Search runs locally. This version does not integrate Analytics, AdSense, or contact-form submission; the policy pages describe the current setup.
- AdSense makes approval decisions at its discretion; this site does not promise approval.
